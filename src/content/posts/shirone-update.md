I moved Miikka’s Archive from Mizuki to [Shirone](https://github.com/LyraVoid/Shirone) after the original theme was archived. Shirone is a rewrite, so I brought my posts, photo albums, and site settings over to a new theme.

The homepage now goes straight to the posts. I took out the large banner and wallpaper, kept the solid light and dark backgrounds, and made a new favicon with an M and a small drawer pull.

The old links to posts and albums still work. The music player is in the sidebar, and the Live2D widget is back on desktop. I kept the original Mizuki version in the site’s Git history.
